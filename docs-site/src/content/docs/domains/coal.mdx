**Use cases**: track historical coal production by state and mine type;
analyze coal shipments from mines to power plants; monitor export/import
trends and pricing; assess remaining reserves and mine capacity.

## Sub-routes (13)

| Sub-route | Summary | Typical frequency | Date range |
|---|---|---|---|
| `aggregate-production` | Aggregate U.S. coal production | annual | 1949 → |
| `consumption-and-quality` | Coal consumption and quality | annual | 1949 → |
| `exports-imports-quantity-price` | Exports/imports quantity and price | annual | 1949 → |
| `market-sales-price` | Market sales price | annual | 1979 → |
| `mine-production` | Mine-level production | annual | 1979 → |
| `price-by-rank` | Coal price by rank | annual | 1979 → |
| `reserves-capacity` | Reserves and capacity | annual | 1974 → |
| `shipments/by-mine-by-plant` | Mine → plant shipments (detail) | annual | — |
| `shipments/mine-aggregates` | Mine → plant aggregates | annual | — |
| `shipments/mine-state-aggregates` | Mine → state aggregates | annual | — |
| `shipments/plant-aggregates` | Plant aggregates | annual | — |
| `shipments/plant-state-aggregates` | Plant → state aggregates | annual | — |
| `shipments/receipts` | Plant receipts | annual | — |

All coal routes are **annual-only**; `frequency=monthly` is rejected by
upstream.

## Agent guidance

- **Aggregate vs mine-level**: `aggregate-production` has no mine detail.
  For mine-level breakdowns use `mine-production` with the `mineId` or
  `mineState` facet.
- **Mine-level data starts later** than the aggregate series (1979 vs 1949).
- **Facet ids are camelCase**: `stateRegionId`, `coalRankId`, `mineTypeId`
  (not all-lowercase). Always run `GET /routes/coal/{route}/facet/{id}`
  first to confirm.
