**Tags**: `natural-gas`, `fossil-fuels`, `us`

**Use cases**: track natural gas consumption by sector, state, or end use;
monitor wellhead, city-gate, and retail natural gas prices; analyze
working-gas storage levels; follow pipeline and LNG import/export flows.

## Sub-routes (7)

Natural Gas uses a **2-level route structure**: `{category}/{dataset}` —
e.g. `cons/sum`, `pri/sum`. Always use the full id.

| Sub-route | Summary | Typical frequency | Date range |
|---|---|---|---|
| `cons/sum` | Consumption by end use | annual, monthly | 1973 → |
| `pri/sum` | Price summary (all sectors) | annual, monthly | 1973 → |
| `prod/oilwells` | Production — oilwell counts | annual | 1973 → |
| `stor/wkly` | Weekly working gas storage | **weekly only** | 2010 → |
| `move/impc` | Pipeline imports by country | annual, monthly | 1973 → |
| `enr/sum` | Exploration & reserves summary | annual | 1973 → |
| `sum/lsum` | LNG summary | annual, monthly | 1973 → |

<Aside type="caution">
  The weekly storage route (`stor/wkly`) **only supports `frequency=weekly`**.
  Annual/monthly requests fail upstream. Use ISO date ranges
  (`YYYY-MM-DD`).
</Aside>

## Facets

| Facet id | Casing | Example values |
|---|---|---|
| `duoarea` | lowercase | `"SCA"`, `"NY"`, `"TEX"` (state codes, not full names) |
| `product` | lowercase | `"Residential"`, `"Commercial"`, `"Industrial"` |
| `process` | lowercase | `"Wellhead"`, `"City Gate"`, `"Delivered to Residential"` |

## Agent guidance

- **Sub-route ids are 2-level**: use `"pri/sum"`, never `"pri"` or `"sum"`.
- **Filter values are ids, not names**: `"SCA"` for Southern California, not
  `"CALIFORNIA"`. Always hit `/facet/{id}` first to map name → id.
- **For prices, narrow by `process` facet** — without it you get a
  cross-product of wellhead / city-gate / delivered prices.
