**Tags**: `aggregate`, `national`, `consumption`, `production`, `us`

**Use cases**: track total U.S. energy production and consumption;
analyze net energy imports/exports; monitor energy intensity over time.

## Sub-routes (1)

| Sub-route | Summary | Typical frequency | Date range |
|---|---|---|---|
| `data` | Total energy data (all records) | monthly | 1949 → |

Flat domain — one route, full dataset.

## Facets

| Facet id | Casing | Example values |
|---|---|---|
| `seriesId` | camelCase | MSN series codes (e.g. `"ELETPUS"` for US total electricity primary consumption) |

<Aside type="caution">
  The **only** facet on this domain is `seriesId` (and aliases). Facets
  live at `/routes/total-energy/facet/{id}` — **not** under the `/data`
  path.
</Aside>

## Worked example

```bash
# US total monthly electricity primary consumption
curl -X POST http://localhost:3000/query \
  -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d '{
    "domain": "total-energy",
    "route": "data",
    "data": ["value"],
    "frequency": "monthly",
    "start": "2020-01",
    "end": "2024-12",
    "facets": { "msn": ["ELETPUS"] },
    "length": 100
  }'
```

## Agent guidance

- **`seriesId` is the workhorse facet** — pick the right MSN code for the
  metric you want. Listing `/routes/total-energy/facet/msn` gives the full
  catalogue.
- Total Energy is a **flat dataset**: monthly U.S. aggregates by MSN code.
  Pair it with [SEDS](/domains/seds/) or [STEO](/domains/steo/) when you
  need state-level or forecast data.
